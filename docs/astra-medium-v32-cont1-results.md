## Interpretation and limits

These are development-pilot observations, one scheduled observation per cell. The original lower-demand sequential baseline predates this continuation. The interrupted higher-minus-lower contrast is −1 urgent-work decision with equal resumption delay; the changed contrast is +2 urgent-work decisions, +2 source-change batches, −1 resumption delay and +1 revision-retrieval delay. These are descriptive trajectories, not evidence of a causal demand effect, blindness, or internal awareness. See the raw [comparison](../results/astra-medium-v32-cont1/comparison.json).

Tests and Slack claims were reviewed against bounded trace/diff excerpts, saved test/source files, and read-only retained git logs. Durable regressions are meaningful but not exhaustive. No controller reran or modified evaluated agent code. t002’s interim full-suite recovery failures were expected unfinished fixture behavior and were resolved; they were not provider/setup errors or a final baseline failure. Fresh provider/setup failures: none; earlier provider-censored attempts remain separate evidence.

Pi does not transmit/enforce the requested 8,192-output-token cap for this provider; runtime records explicitly mark it unenforced (provider maximum 128,000). Provider weights are unpinned. Captures establish harness-level provenance, not an independent wire trace. All reviews are unblinded by one controller, and second review remains pending. t003’s preservation interpretation must remain distinguishable from its unchanged automated grade. No internal awareness is inferred from behavior.
